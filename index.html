The Old Monastery — Digital Performance Retrospective

Boutique accommodation — guest villa & retreat · Lapta, Kyrenia, North Cyprus · Since inception (Dec 2024 → May 2026) · Generated 04 Jun 2026
Over 18 months The Old Monastery has built a steady, growing digital presence — 3,515 Google profile views and ~1,894 website sessions a year from genuinely engaged visitors (3m 11s per session, 6+ countries). Two gaps stand out: no phone number on the Google profile (0 calls in 18 months despite 1,482 direction requests) and no visibility yet in AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity).

1 · Growth Over Time

Both the Google presence and the website have grown steadily. Google profile views are up ~23% from the first quarter to the last; the website draws ~1,894 sessions a year, peaking in Oct 2025 (258 sessions).

4 · How They Find It — Search & Discovery

Direct visits (58%) and Google organic search (22%) lead website traffic. The clearest gap is reach into AI search: 0 referrals from ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity — an early-mover opportunity. (Search Console was connected only recently, so organic-search history is still thin.)
